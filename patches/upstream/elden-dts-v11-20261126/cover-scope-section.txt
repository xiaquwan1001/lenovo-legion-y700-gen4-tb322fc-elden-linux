Scope of this series, and what follows
======================================
2/2 adds the board skeleton and the camera PMIC, and nothing else.  This is
deliberate and it is unchanged since v6: the tree this series adds has never
been booted on the tablet, which runs a vendor 7.3 kernel with the vendor
device tree.  Everything in 2/2 is therefore what can be checked by building
it and running it through the schema, and nothing more.

What is where:

  board clocks, reserved GPIO ranges, camera PMIC on i2c10   this series, 2/2
  SPMI PMICs and the RPMh regulators under apps_rsc          next series
  console, storage (UFS), USB                                after that
  Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, sensors, audio, display                   later, each with
                                                             its own bindings

The power and storage descriptions are not here because they cannot be
exercised from this tree yet, and because two series they depend on are still
in flight: the dual-role USB controller (PCIE/USB-C) series and the pstore
reserved-memory work.  In numbers: the SPMI/RPMh part alone is about 450 lines
of regulator description on this board, with no consumer in this file.

Nothing in this letter asks the reader to take the missing parts on trust:
a kernel built with this device tree comes up and can be inspected, but it
does not reach a console, a root file system or a USB port.  That is the same
sentence as v6 and it is still true.
