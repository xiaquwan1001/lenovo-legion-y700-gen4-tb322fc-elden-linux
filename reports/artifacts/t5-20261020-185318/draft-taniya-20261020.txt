Sorry for the delay - here is the data you asked for on 2026-10-13, together
with the method used to collect it, because the method matters for one of the

Hardware: Lenovo Legion Tab Y700 Gen4 (TB322FC), SM8750P.
Kernel: 7.3.0-rc7-debug, mainline based, no vendor patches touching clk/genpd.

Sequence, in the order you requested
------------------------------------
1. modprobe the pair: camcc-sm8750 + cambistmclkcc-sm8750 (both loaded, 19 camera
   modules total, 1 media node, 17 video nodes) - i.e. this is the Q24 state, not
   a "camera absent" state.
2. dmesg (complete, first line t=0.000000, 970 lines).
3. pm_genpd_summary (read first, cheap) - 116 lines.
4. clk_summary (read last) - 804 lines.
5. one real display blank from userspace: org.gnome.ScreenSaver.SetActive(true),
   DSI DPMS On -> Off. No s2idle, no system suspend.
6. wake via SetActive(false), then dmesg/pm_genpd/clk_summary again.

Caveat on the clk_summary/genpd snapshots
----------------------------------------
Joonhoe Kim pointed out on the list that reading clk_summary itself runtime-
resumes the clock controllers, so the state you read is not necessarily the
state at fault time. We confirm that on this board: the act of reading
clk_summary emitted a new dmesg line at t=4152.99
  wsa_macro 6b00000.codec: using zero-initialized flat cache, ...
So please read the clk_summary as "state right after an explicit resume of the
clock controllers", and the dmesg as the authoritative timeline.

Result of this run (please note: no reset this time)
----------------------------------------------------
With this 7.3-rc7 kernel, camera controllers paired, and one real blank, the SoC
survived - boot_id unchanged across the whole run, ssh reachable throughout,
desktop rendered again on wake. dmesg grew by 10 lines only: the two DSI links
re-programming their timing on blank/resume and the touch panel re-downloading
its firmware. The instant hard reset we reported in the v3 thread was observed
on our 7.2-lineage kernel; on 7.3-rc7 we cannot reproduce it with this exact
sequence (n=1 so far). I would rather tell you that than dress the data up.

What the snapshots show (camera stack loaded, idle, before the blank)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- cam_cc_titan_top_gdsc          on        (all of its child GDSCs off-0)
- ac7b000.cci / ac7c000.cci      active    (both CCI instances, no children)
- mmcx                           on        (320 children)
- cx                             on        (320 children)
- cam_bist_mclk_cc_pll0          rate 960000000 Hz, enable_count 0

And during the blank (genpd diff, 38 lines; clk diff, 96 lines):
- cam_cc_titan_top_gdsc          on  -> off-0
- ac7b000.cci / ac7c000.cci      active -> suspended
- mdss_gdsc                      on  -> off-0
- gcc_ufs_phy_gdsc children      64  -> 0

The interesting detail for our question 1/2 is the first block: the camera
Titan top GDSC is on while every child GDSC under it is off-0, and both CCI
devices are runtime active while the camera clocks have enable_count 0. That is
the "half powered" state we suspect, and it is exactly the state in which the
v7.2 kernel died on blank. The BIST MCLK PLL is programmed to 960 MHz with no
enable.

Our four questions from v3 are still open (your reply did not address them, and
we would rather have your view than guess):
1. Is cambistmclkcc meant to be usable as a bare clock controller with no GDSC
   and no consumer, or does the camcc side have to stay powered while the camera
   BIST block keeps its AHB clock and PLL programmed?
2. Is there a documented ordering constraint between the camera BIST block and
   display power/clock collapse (dispcc) on the shared MMCX/MX domains? On the
   v7.2 kernel any display power transition with both controllers loaded took
   the SoC down instantly; we cannot see that interaction in the clock or genpd
   code paths.
3. Which reset-reason source exists on SM8750 that Linux can read after such an
   event? PON latches nothing and there is no pstore here. Is there an AOP or
   CPUCp crash/health register, or a smem scatter area, that distinguishes
   "PS_HOLD dropped" from "cold reset"?
4. Until the root cause is known, what default would you recommend for
   distributions: an always-on vote for cambistmclkcc, a required vdd_mx supply
   binding, or deferring the cambistmclkcc probe until the display is off?
